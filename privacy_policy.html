Privacy Policy for Padee

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

🔒 Short Summary

Padee is built with your privacy as our core priority. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, location data, or usage metrics. Everything stays 100% on your local device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Padee mobile application ("App"), developed by AlphaDog ("we", "our", or "us"). Padee is designed as an offline digital bead counter for prayer, mindfulness, and meditation.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We believe in absolute user privacy:

  • No Personal Information: We do not require account registration, email addresses, names, or phone numbers.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not embed any third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to track your behavior.
  • No Advertising: The app contains no advertising networks or ad identifiers.
  • Local Storage: Your preferences (bead count, language preference, vibration settings, round goals) are stored exclusively in your device's local memory and are never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Device Permissions

Padee only requests the minimal device access needed to perform its utility functions:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback when you tap a bead or reach your completed round goal. You can toggle vibration off at any time in the app settings.

Padee does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Padee operates fully offline and does not integrate with any external third-party SDKs, APIs, or data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Padee does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Padee is safe and suitable for all age groups.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect operational or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
